## What to actually research

1. **Sourcegraph's real TypeScript/Angular-specific indexing — checked directly against this project's own known, measured gap.** Investigate `scip-typescript` directly: does it (or any Sourcegraph product) resolve Angular template bindings — the HTML-template layer where this project's own extractor initially missed 92 of 160 real `formControlName` usages (57%) because it only walked `node.inputs` and silently missed the plain-literal-attribute form? 2. **Sourcegraph's real approach (or lack of one) to UX/design-facing facts — the most important angle of this round.** Does Sourcegraph, Cody, or any adjacent Sourcegraph product ingest anything beyond pure source-code structure — real user-facing screen text, form option labels, design-tool data (Figma or similar), or UI-behavior facts that can't be recovered from AST alone? This project has an honest, long-standing, unsolved gap here (Part A2 above) — a real, current answer either way (yes, they've solved a version of this — or no, real code-intelligence tooling stays code-only industry-wide, even at this maturity level) is genuinely useful, not just a nice-to-have.
3. **Sourcegraph's real approach to cross-repo/cross-language type-mirror divergence.** Given SCIP is explicitly cross-language and cross-repo by design, does Sourcegraph have any real, documented feature (Code Insights, Batch Changes, or similar) for detecting the kind of drift this project found directly — 92 real cross-repo symbol ambiguities between Firebase and Angular, narrowed to 42 genuine ones after manual review? Or does their tooling surface the *connections* (via SCIP's cross-references) without making the *judgment call* this project had to make by hand (safe mirror vs. genuine collision)?
4. **Sourcegraph's real approach to Cody's code-context retrieval — checked against this project's own retrieval-architecture findings.** `02-findings-retrieval-architecture-2026-09-05.md` found that this project's own hybrid-retrieval attempts (BM25-style, trigram-based) both failed, and that real production systems (Claude Code, GitHub Copilot) favor agentic, iterative tool-calling over a single fused retrieval score. Does Sourcegraph publish real, current, technical detail on how Cody actually retrieves code context (their own blog, docs, or engineering talks) — and does it corroborate, extend, or contradict this project's own findings and its resulting MCP-tool-layer + agent-persona architecture (`adr-007.md`)?
5. **Sourcegraph's real AST-walker/extraction-coverage verification practice, if any is published.** `08-...md`'s prior round found this project's own grep-cross-check discipline (verifying extracted-construct counts against real source) is, as far as could be found, not a named practice anywhere in the Swift/Kotlin ecosystem. Check specifically whether Sourcegraph — as a company whose entire product depends on extraction correctness at a much larger scale — publishes anything (blog posts, conference talks, open-source test suites) about how they verify their own indexers' real coverage. If genuinely nothing is found even from a company at this scale, that's an even stronger version of the same honest finding, worth stating plainly.
