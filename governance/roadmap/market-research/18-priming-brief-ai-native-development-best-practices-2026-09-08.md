## What to actually research

2. **Real, current vocabulary for "skill" vs. "persona" vs. "rules" vs. "context"/"grounding" in production agent systems.** Anthropic's own "Skills" concept (Claude Skills), OpenAI's equivalent constructs, Google's Agent Builder/ADK terminology, and any other real, named, current framework's vocabulary for these same four concepts.
